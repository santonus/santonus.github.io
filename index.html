Executive Summary

I work at the intersection of software engineering, dependable computing, and AI-driven system analysis. I have contributed foundational methods in software modularity, system dependability, performance and energy characterization of distributed and parallel systems, and anomaly detection.

I led industrial research programs that translate these ideas into large-scale, deployable systems. My work combines rigorous theoretical and empirical analysis with industrial deployment, resulting in dozens of U.S. patents across software, cloud, AI, and industrial systems.

I guide multidisciplinary teams and students to build resilient cloud-edge services, digital twins for cyber-physical systems, smart mobility solutions, and sustainable computing platforms.
